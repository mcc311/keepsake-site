留影Keepsake

同一個景，只留最好的那幾張Keep the best of every scene

旅行回來，同一個景拍了十張？留影會幫你分好組，你只要挑出想留的，其他的一次刪掉。 Back from a trip with ten shots of the same view? Keepsake groups them for you, so you only pick the ones to keep and delete the rest at once.

  • 挑要留的，不是挑要刪的Pick what to keep

    同一個景、時間相近的照片自動分成一組，每組標出建議保留的那張，最後由你決定。Shots of the same scene are grouped automatically, with a suggested keeper in each group. You make the call.

  • 並排比較Side by side

    兩張並排，放大一張另一張跟著放大。眼睛有沒有閉、焦點有沒有對到，一眼就看出來。Zoom one photo and the other follows, so closed eyes and missed focus are easy to spot.

  • 旅途Trips

    依照片的位置列出你去過的地方，選一段旅程，只整理那幾天的照片。See the places you've been, pick a trip, and sort just those days.

  • 像「照片」App 一樣Feels like Photos

    熟悉的相簿格子、捏合縮放、左右滑動，打開就會用。The familiar album grid, pinch to zoom, swipe between photos. Nothing new to learn.

照片不離開你的 iPhoneYour photos stay on your iPhone

所有分析都在裝置上進行。不上傳、不需要帳號、沒有廣告也不追蹤你。Everything is analyzed on device. No uploads, no account, no ads, no tracking.
