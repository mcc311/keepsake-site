常見問題FAQ

會不會不小心刪到我的照片？Could it delete my photos by accident?

不會。留影不會自己刪任何照片，每次刪除都要你按下按鈕、再經過系統確認。刪掉的照片會先進「照片」App 的「最近刪除」，30 天內都能救回來。No. Keepsake never deletes on its own: every deletion needs your tap plus a system confirmation, and deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

TestFlight 是什麼？安全嗎？What is TestFlight? Is it safe?

TestFlight 是 Apple 官方的測試平台，測試版一樣要經過 Apple 審核才能發出。測試版會在 90 天後過期，到時候會有新版本或正式版。TestFlight is Apple's official beta platform, and beta builds are reviewed by Apple before they go out. Each build expires after 90 days, by which time there will be a newer one or the App Store release.
